## Overview

**Graph Theory Research, Gettysburg College · Spring 2024 · Advisor: Prof. Nathan Shank**

I defined two new parametric graph families — **Firecracker graphs** and **Tollbooth graphs** — using formal vertex-edge constructions, and proved closed-form results about their structural properties.

## Research Question

What structural properties characterize these novel graph families, and can we derive exact, closed-form results for the classical invariants — domination number, Eulerian properties, diameter, and independence number?

## Approach

- Constructed the Firecracker and Tollbooth families through **formal vertex-edge definitions**, parameterized to capture infinite classes of graphs.
- Proved **closed-form theorems** for domination number, Eulerian properties, graph diameter, and independence number across the families.
- Developed the work into the research manuscript *"Firecrackers and Tollbooth Graphs: Properties of Novel Graph Structures"* (Gettysburg College, 2024).

## My Contribution

The project was mine from definition to proof: I formulated the graph families, conjectured the results, and proved the closed-form characterizations under Prof. Shank's advising.

## Results

A set of proven structural results on two novel graph families, written up as an undergraduate research manuscript. The work trained me in the discipline the rest of my research leans on: **precise definitions, rigorous argument, and comfort with abstraction**.

## What This Motivated

My theoretical foundation. When I now work on search, optimization, and reasoning in AI systems, I draw on the same habits — formalize the structure first, then prove (or empirically establish) what follows from it.
