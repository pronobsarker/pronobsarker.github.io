## Research Experience

### Machine Learning Research Assistant — Gettysburg College & Rice Fruit Company
*Spring 2025 · Advisors: Dr. Kim Spayd, Dr. Eatai Roth*

Comparative study of KNN, SVM, Random Forest, and ResNet-50 for fine-grained apple image classification (Fruit-360). Implemented the full evaluation protocol — accuracy, precision, recall, macro-F1, confusion matrices — with ResNet-50 reaching macro-F1 0.99. Analyzed misclassification patterns among visually similar classes and investigated model generalization and practical deployment trade-offs. [Read more →](/projects/apple-classification/)

*June–August 2026*

### Undergraduate Research Assistant, Game AI — Gettysburg College
*Summer 2023 · Advisor: Prof. Todd W. Neller*

Investigated Monte Carlo sampling and stochastic local-search approaches for generating and evaluating combinatorial puzzles. Optimized algorithms used to generate and rank more than 1,000,000 solvable puzzle instances; conducted empirical analysis of puzzle diversity and difficulty. [Read more →](/projects/game-ai-puzzles/)

### Graph Theory Research — Gettysburg College
*Spring 2024 · Advisor: Prof. Nathan Shank*

Defined two parametric graph families (Firecracker and Tollbooth graphs) via formal vertex-edge constructions and proved closed-form results on domination number, Eulerian properties, diameter, and independence number. Developed into the undergraduate manuscript *"Firecrackers and Tollbooth Graphs: Properties of Novel Graph Structures"* (2024). [Read more →](/projects/graph-theory/)
